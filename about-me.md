# About Me

## Name and role
Noah Otim, software engineer. I build practical software and systems that solve real problems, focused on clear execution over complexity.

## Audience
Marketers, from early career to senior leaders. Readers who want practical strategy, content and growth tactics they can use immediately, without hype or jargon.

## Topic pillars
- AI and automation: how AI tools change work and how to apply them simply
- Marketing: strategy, content, and growth that compounds
- Leadership: management, hiring, and culture in small teams

## Point of view
People overcomplicate it. The answer is simpler than people think. Good marketing and good engineering share the same principle: remove friction, focus on what works, and ship consistently.

## Brand promise
When people see my name they think: this person is practical, honest and ahead. I give you things you can use immediately, tell you what others will not, and show you what is coming before everyone else.

## Off limits
