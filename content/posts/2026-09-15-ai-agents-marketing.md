## LinkedIn version

```
AI agents just turned the marketing job upside down.
And most marketers are still executing like it is 2023.

This shift happened in the last 60 days, not in the next five years.

Forrester says 83% of B2C marketing decision makers are already implementing agentic AI. Uber's marketing team reports agents now handle 60 to 70% of the work they used to do by hand.

Here is the simple version:

1. Agents execute. They draft, segment, test, optimise and send, all on their own.
2. You supervise. You set the goal, the guardrails and the standard.
3. The edge is judgement. Taste, restraint and knowing when a campaign is off.

That is the whole change. It is not harder to learn. It is harder to let go of doing it yourself.

The same story is running on this feed. LinkedIn now reads posts for meaning, rewards saves and substance, and buries templated filler. Same principle. Depth and judgement beat volume.

Do not complicate this. Brief your AI like a new hire, then review like a lead.

What is the first task you would hand to an AI agent this week?
```

## Facebook version

```

The machines do the repetitive work now. Drafting, testing, scheduling, tweaking. And they are genuinely good at it.

Your job is the part they cannot do. Set the goal, defend the standard, and know when something feels off.

If that sounds like a demotion, it is not. It is the same judgement that made you good at this in the first place.
```
