Growth, not volume

5 skills that actually grow your account in 2026

1
Hook first, question never
A specific claim beats a question. Story and contrarian hooks outperform every other opener.
2
Chase saves, not likes
Saves are the strongest signal in the 2026 algorithm. Build frameworks people bookmark.
3
One idea per post
Single-idea posts pull about 2.7x more comments than multi-topic posts. Depth beats breadth.
4
Lead with a number
A concrete figure in the first 160 characters lifts engagement. Specificity beats adjectives.
5
Keep the link out
External links in the body cut reach by up to 70%. Link lives in the first comment.
