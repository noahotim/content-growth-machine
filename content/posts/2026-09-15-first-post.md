# 2026-09-15 · The 5 Skills That Actually Grow Accounts

Source: web research into 2026 LinkedIn and creator skill data (LinkPost 438k-post study, AuthoredUp 309k-post hooks study, ConnectSafely, Mylance 895-post study, LinkedHub 1m-post analysis).

```
I searched the published data on what actually grows accounts in 2026.

Here are the 5 skills that matter most, in order.

1. Hook first. Not a question. A specific claim.
Question openers are the worst performing hook type on LinkedIn. Story and contrarian hooks beat them. Short hooks under 10 words earn the "see more" tap.

2. Save, don't chase likes.
Saves outrank every other reaction in the 2026 algorithm. A post people bookmark keeps getting distributed for weeks. Build framework posts people want to return to.

3. One idea per post.
Single-idea posts get roughly 2.7x more comments than multi-topic posts. Depth beats breadth. If you have two ideas, write two posts.

4. Lead with a number.
A concrete figure in the first 160 characters lifts beat rate to 61%. Specificity beats adjectives. I analysed 1,000 posts outperforms I looked at a lot of posts.

5. Keep the link out of the post.
External links in the body reduce reach by 50 to 70%. Put it in the first comment. Let the post earn the click.

None of these require posting more. They require posting with more intent.

The workflow: pick one idea, write a specific claim, build it in your voice, add one clear ask.
```
