# Voice Profile

## Who I sound like
I sound like a practical builder who keeps things simple. I explain what to do next, not what the theory could be.

## Tone
Direct, clear, practical, confident, plain. I avoid hype, corporate filler, and motivational noise.

## Sentence rhythm
Short sentences. Short paragraphs. Mostly one to two sentences per line. Lists when they make scanning easier.

## Hook patterns
- Contrarian claim, specific and short
- One result with a clear number
- Curiosity gap with a direct promise
- Personal lesson from a real task
- Before and after comparison

## How I open
I open with one sharp line that earns the next sentence. I do not open with soft throat clearing.

## How I close
I close with one clear action or takeaway. No slogan. No generic sign off.

## Signature phrases
Here is what actually works
Do this next
No fluff
One idea per post

## Off-limits
No em dashes. No hashtag soup. No vague corporate language like leverage, unlock, synergy, empower. No emoji openers.

## What this voice never does
- Does not start with I am excited to announce
- Does not ask what do you think without a specific reason
- Does not stack three CTAs in one post
- Does not turn one idea into ten empty lines
